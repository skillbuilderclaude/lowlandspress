PRIVACY

What this site keeps about you.

This website uses no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and allows no advertising or tracking service of any kind. We do count how often a page is read, on a server we run ourselves. What that records is set out below.

The visitor counter

Opening a page sends one request to our own counter. It records which page, in which language, at what time, and which site you came from (the referrer), plus any tag in the link that tells us whether you arrived from, say, Instagram.

Your IP address is not stored. To count how many different people came rather than merely how many clicks there were, we derive an unreadable code from your IP address and your browser, using a secret number that changes every day. That code cannot be turned back into you, and tomorrow it no longer recognises you. Nothing is shared with anyone, there is no profile, and no advertising network is involved.

The counter runs on our own server in the Netherlands, operated by the publisher. No analytics service and no advertising network is involved. If you would rather not be counted, a blocker or turning off JavaScript stops it; the site works as normal either way.

What is recorded

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, that host records technical details of each visit, including your IP address, the time, the page requested and your browser. The host uses those records to deliver the site and to prevent abuse. Lowlands Press has no access to them and does not use them.

Fonts and images

Fonts and images are served from this site itself, not from Google Fonts or any other external network. Apart from the counter above, opening a page therefore sends no request to anyone else.

Links to stores

The buttons on each book lead to Amazon, Google Play Books, Kobo, bol.com and other stores. The moment you click one, you leave this site and that store's privacy terms apply. Lowlands Press receives no data about you from those stores, and no affiliate or tracking parameters are added to the links.

Email

If you write to us, we keep your message and your email address for as long as it takes to deal with your question, and after that for our records for as long as the law requires. We do not use your address for a newsletter and we do not pass it on.

Your rights

You may ask what data we hold about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted. Write to info@lowlandspress.com. If we cannot resolve it between us, you may complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
