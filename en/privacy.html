PRIVACY

What this site keeps about you.

This website collects no personal data of its own. There are no forms on it, no advertising or analytics cookies, and no third-party trackers. You can read the site without leaving anything behind.

What is recorded

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, that host records technical details of each visit, including your IP address, the time, the page requested and your browser. The host uses those records to deliver the site and to prevent abuse. Lowlands Press has no access to them and does not use them.

Fonts and images

Fonts and images are served from this site itself, not from Google Fonts or any other external network. Opening a page therefore sends no request to a third party.

Links to stores

The buttons on each book lead to Amazon, Google Play Books, Kobo, bol.com and other stores. The moment you click one, you leave this site and that store's privacy terms apply. Lowlands Press receives no data about you from those stores, and no affiliate or tracking parameters are added to the links.

Email

If you write to us, we keep your message and your email address for as long as it takes to deal with your question, and after that for our records for as long as the law requires. We do not use your address for a newsletter and we do not pass it on.

Your rights

You may ask what data we hold about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted. Write to info@lowlandspress.com. If we cannot resolve it between us, you may complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
